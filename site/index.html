The property

It doesn't look new. It doesn't need to.

The Zarea hotel has taken guests since 1981. It doesn't look new. It doesn't need to. What's for sale now is the whole enterprise: the building, the land it stands on, and the hotel still running inside.

Behind Bulevardul Ștefan cel Mare, on its own forecourt under mature trees, the tower keeps a low entrance annex and heats itself independently, with its own boiler house and full utility connections. None of it is idle: 137 rooms, a 30-seat conference room and a 50-seat café are all in daily use.

Twelve floors, one plot, in the centre of the capital, all ready for renovation into a hotel, offices, or a mix of both.

Enterprise founded in 1992. 100% state-owned, administered by the Public Property Agency. IDNO 1003600080200.

Location

In the heart of Chișinău

Four minutes on foot from Bulevardul Ștefan cel Mare, the city's main artery and a key public transport corridor. The National Bank, Parliament, the Presidency and Government House are all within a fifteen-minute walk, and Chișinău International Airport is around 15 km away.
